How We Work Together

A streamlined 4-step workflow to turn your raw video files into viral polished content.

01 / BRIEF

Raw Footage & Concept

Send over your raw footage, video references, and target channel requirements via Google Drive or Frame.io.

02 / EDIT

Assembly & Pacing

Crafting the storytelling narrative, cutting filler pauses, adding hooks, and building the initial edit structure.

03 / POLISH

Graphics, Sound & Color

Applying 2D/3D motion graphics, mixing sound design FX, syncing music, and executing DaVinci color grading.

04 / DELIVERY

Review & Final Render

Reviewing feedback, adjusting revisions, and delivering master 4K / 1080p files optimized for YouTube or Socials.
